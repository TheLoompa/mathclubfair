    05 What we actually do

    Problems worth arguing about

    Olympiad-flavoured puzzles, weird proofs, and the occasional problem nobody in the room can crack. Yet.

    Contest prep, together

    Mock rounds and past papers for national & school olympiads — with people who'll explain the step you missed.

    Maths you can see

    Origami, fractals, code, paper models, π-day chaos. If it can be built or drawn, we build or draw it.

    No gatekeeping

    You don't need to be "a maths person". Curiosity is the whole entry requirement. Class 7 to 12, all welcome.

    Come for the puzzles, stay for the people who get excited about the same nonsense you do.
